1What it is

An open redirect happens when a site sends users to an address taken from the link, without checking it. The link starts on a trusted site, so people trust it. The attacker only changes the address at the end to point to their own page.

4Why it matters

    
Attackers use trusted links in phishing emails. The victim sees the real shop address, logs in, and lands on a fake page that steals their details. Email filters also trust the real domain, so these links pass through.
Real incident: in 2021 Microsoft warned about a large phishing campaign that used open redirect links on trusted sites to send victims to fake login pages.
