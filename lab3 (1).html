1What it is

A shop multiplies the price by whatever quantity it receives and never checks that the number makes sense. A quantity of -5 gives a negative total, so the shop owes the customer money. This is a business logic flaw (OWASP A04:2021 Insecure Design, CWE-840).

4Why it matters

    
Attackers can get free goods, or even make the shop pay them, using nothing but a normal request with a strange number. Scanners rarely catch it, because nothing looks broken.
Real case: CVE-2024-50968 describes a quantity flaw in a small open-source shopping system (itsourcecode Agri-Trading Online Shopping System 1.0). Sending "-0" as the quantity made the total price zero, so items could be checked out for free.
